Amer Fort crowd management

Smart tourism, built for the iStart Rajasthan CHANGE Challenge

Question
How many visitors can a heritage site hold safely at once?
Approach
Safe capacity is set by the narrowest points on the visitor route, such as the gates, the ascent ramp and the lake bridge, using pedestrian-flow mechanics. Site-area estimates overstate it.
Built
A crowd-counting pipeline (YOLOv8 and CSRNet) validated on real fort footage, a live dashboard with threshold alerts, and a visitor slot-booking app.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, React, Supabase, Google Earth measurements

MCH RiskTrack
